Host questions

Good to know.

Is Everpost free to use?

Yes. Creating an invitation, sharing the link, downloading the card and collecting guest replies are all free.

Do my guests need an account?

Guests can open the link in their browser. They can send their RSVP or wish through WhatsApp or copy it into a message.

Can I use the design for New Year’s Eve?

Yes. The Evergreen Letter works for both holidays. Change the title, date and message to suit your celebration.

How do I send my invitation?

After creating your invitation, copy its guest link or choose Share on WhatsApp. You can also download a PNG to send alongside the link.

Where can I see RSVPs and wishes?

Guests send their replies directly to you. Check your WhatsApp conversation or the messaging app where you shared the invitation.

Can guests add the party to their calendar?

Yes. The invitation includes a Google Calendar link and an .ics file for Apple Calendar, Outlook and other calendar apps.

Who can see my invitation?

Anyone with the full link can open it. Your event details travel in the link, so share it only with your guests.
