Good to know

How do guests receive it?

Share the invitation text on WhatsApp, or download the card as a high-resolution PNG and send or print it. In the guest preview, guests open a sealed envelope, see the countdown, add the party to their calendar and RSVP in two taps.

Can I print the card?

Yes — every design is a 5 × 7 inch card. Download the PNG and print it at home or at a print shop, or post the same image to social media.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a design, type your details and everything updates live on the card. Nothing to install and no sign-up to start.

Christmas or New Year — or both?

There are five designs for each occasion. Switching occasion in the picker also updates the sample wording, date and time so you can see a realistic card straight away.
